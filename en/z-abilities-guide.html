Z Abilities guide for Dragon Ball Legends

Z Abilities are one of the biggest — and most misunderstood — power levers in Dragon Ball Legends. Here is how they work, how they spread across your team and how to get the most out of them.

Updated September 16, 2026 · 3 min read

What is a Z Ability?

A Z Ability is a passive bonus attached to every character. Unlike their battle cards, it works as soon as the character is part of your team: it raises one or more stats (Health, ATK, DEF, Damage Inflicted…) for the team members who meet its condition — usually a shared tag.

That’s why a team is never just the sum of its characters’ kits: a mediocre fighter can be an excellent Z Ability carrier if it hands out big bonuses to a category you play.

Levels: I, II, III and IV

A Z Ability unlocks in levels from I to IV. Each level strengthens the previous one; level IV is the maximum. In DBL Optimizer, the level is set in the panel that unfolds when you hover a card (under the card on mobile), so you can simulate a team that isn’t fully unlocked yet.

Key point: levels don’t stack. If level III gives “+30% Health” and level IV gives “+40% Health”, the level you reached (40%) counts — not the sum of both. For every stat, the tool keeps the highest unlocked value.

Zenkai Z Abilities

A character that has been Zenkai Awakened unlocks an extra Z Ability, separate from its regular one. Both add up: a Zenkai character brings its original Z Ability and its Zenkai Z Ability. The optimizer counts the Zenkai Z Ability at its maximum. That is what makes Zenkai units such sought-after team supports.

Conditions: tags, episodes, colors

Most Z Abilities only benefit characters that meet a condition, based on the game’s labels:

  • Color — Red, Yellow, Purple, Green, Blue, Light;
  • Class tags — Saiyan, Son Family, Fusion Warrior…;
  • Episode tags — movies, sagas…;
  • Character tags — Goku, Vegeta…;
  • Battle style — Melee type, Defense type….

In practice, a Z Ability giving “+40% Strike ATK to Saiyans” only boosts the Saiyans in your team. Building a team whose characters share the same tags lets every Z Ability benefit everyone: that is the heart of optimization. The optimizer’s “Team tags” table shows at a glance which tags are shared, and by whom.

Propagation to teammates

A Z Ability belongs to one character but reaches the whole team. For each teammate, the condition is checked line by line: part of a Z Ability can apply to one character and not to another.

The Z Ability Tree in DBL Optimizer shows exactly that: an arrow goes from the targeted character to each teammate, green if 100% of the Z Ability is received, yellow if only part of it applies, red if nothing does. It’s the quickest way to spot a carrier whose bonuses are wasted.

The Leader privilege

The Leader (★) is treated differently in the calculation:

  • as a receiver, the Leader gets every Z Ability in the team unconditionally;
  • as a giver, the Leader’s Z Ability applies unconditionally to their two trio teammates; other characters still need to meet its usual conditions.

Changing the Leader can therefore transform a team’s summary. In the tool, set the Leader with the star, or click “Disable leader” to compare with a team without this privilege. In Proud mode, the privilege follows the trio the Leader actually fights with — one of the things the Trio C analysis checks.

How Z Abilities combine with equipment

Z Ability and equipment bonuses land in the same calculation layers (base, pure, direct), which multiply each other. A big “base” Z Ability therefore amplifies the “pure” lines of your equipment. Details are in the equipment guide.

How to optimize your Z Abilities

  1. Group tags. The more tags your characters share, the more widely conditional Z Abilities apply.
  2. Favor Zenkai carriers. Two Z Abilities beat one.
  3. Pick the right Leader — ideally the one whose Z Ability would be most blocked by its trio teammates’ conditions.
  4. Check coverage with the Z Ability Tree: a yellow or red arrow is lost potential.
