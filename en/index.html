How to use DBL Optimizer

Four steps to find out, with numbers, whether your team holds up — and where it loses power.

  1. Build your two trios

    Click an empty slot to pick a character from 500+ cards, filtered by rarity, color, name or tag. Pick your Leader with the ★ star.

  2. Set Z Abilities and equipment

    Hover a character to pick their Z Ability level (I to IV) and 3 pieces of equipment. Compatible equipment is suggested by default, with full details.

  3. Check synergies

    The tag table shows who carries which tag, and “Team composition” tells you which equipment effects are active in each trio — or what is missing to trigger them.

  4. Read the summaries

    Z Ability summary, global Z + equipment summary per character, Z Ability propagation tree and, in Proud mode, a Trio C analysis with the best possible trio.
