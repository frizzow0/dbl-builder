Where to start?

New to DBL Optimizer? Start with Getting started: it walks through every part of the tool. The Z Abilities and equipment guides then explain the two bonus sources the optimizer calculates, and why they multiply each other.

Playing Proud mode? The Trio C guide shows why two strong trios don’t always make a strong deciding match, and how to check it before you play.

How these guides are written

Each guide describes how DBL Optimizer actually calculates: the rules it applies, their limits and how to read the results. Character and equipment data is updated regularly; the latest update is from September 25, 2026. Spotted a mistake or a mechanic that is described wrongly? Let us know.
